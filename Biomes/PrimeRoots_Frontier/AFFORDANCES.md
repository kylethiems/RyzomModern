# Biome Affordances: Prime Roots Frontier

## Executable Ecological Responses

### 1. `MobilizeKitinPatrol`
- **Trigger:** Elevated distress signals (>0.40) from adjacent logged biomes.
- **Action:** Sends Level 45 Armored Kitin Harvesters along subterranean root tunnels to surface at the deforested border.
- **Impact:** Converts the safe logging zone into a high-danger combat territory.

### 2. `ApexCarnivoreMigration`
- **Trigger:** Prey displacement from village forces herbivores into the buffer perimeter.
- **Action:** Shifts Apex Kincher and Varinx packs 200m inward, pursuing displaced Yubo directly toward the starting village gates.
- **Impact:** Low-level starting players encounter deadly high-level predators if the village is aggressively clear-cut.

### 3. `SubterraneanBurrowRetreat`
- **Trigger:** Distress signals drop to zero and prey disperses evenly.
- **Action:** Apex predators retreat to deep Prime Roots chasms.

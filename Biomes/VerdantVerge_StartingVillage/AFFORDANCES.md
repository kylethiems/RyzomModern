# Biome Affordances: Verdant Verge

## Executable Ecological Responses

### 1. `DisplaceFaunaToPeriphery`
- **Trigger:** Canopy integrity dropped or active felling vibration.
- **Action:** Forces grazing herds (Yubo, Mektoub) to retreat 150m-300m radially outward into frontier border zones.
- **Side Effect:** Clears starting village of docile game, starving local beginner hunters.

### 2. `SignalEcosystemDistress`
- **Trigger:** Cumulative harvested timber exceeds sustainable yield threshold (>35% deforestation).
- **Action:** Broadcasts chemical pheromone pulse and root vibrations across subterranean mycorrhizal network to adjacent biomes (`PrimeRoots_Frontier`).
- **Pheromone Signature:** High-frequency distress pulse attracting carnivorous predators and Kitin wardens.

### 3. `RegrowSaplings`
- **Trigger:** Deforestation halted for >12 world hours, sap pressure positive.
- **Action:** Sprouts rapid Swanson-curve pioneer saplings, restoring 2.5% canopy integrity per game day.

### 4. `SustainPeacefulCanopy`
- **Trigger:** Nominal disturbance (<15%), balanced fauna grazing.
- **Action:** Retains native herds in peaceful village center grazing nodes.

# Registered Affordance Methods: Foreman Dax

These methods represent the discrete behavioral interactions Dax can dynamically execute. His lightweight topological neural map rearranges their activation utility based on real-time environmental stimuli.

## Method 1: AppraiseFelledTimber
- **Trigger Stimuli:** `TimberHarvested`, `LogInspected`
- **Action:** Calculates board-feet volume and offers cash buy-order based on residual stumpage valuation.
- **Dialogue:** *"By the Great Root! That's {volume} MBF of {grade}. I'll buy it for ${profit} delivered to the Pyr outpost!"*

## Method 2: DispatchMektoubCaravan
- **Trigger Stimuli:** `HighStumpage`, `CaravanAvailable`
- **Action:** Routes pack beasts to haul timber logs across rugged mountain paths.
- **Dialogue:** *"Rigging harness on two heavy Mektoubs. Haul cost is ${haul}/MBF for the {km}km transit."*

## Method 3: WarnKitinPheromones
- **Trigger Stimuli:** `DeforestationHigh (>40%)`, `GroundTremor`
- **Action:** Issues emergency environmental caution and halts harvesting.
- **Dialogue:** *"Halt the blades! The soil is trembling. You've stripped too much root bark—the Kitin swarm is stirring!"*

## Method 4: SoundOutpostAlarm
- **Trigger Stimuli:** `KitinSwarmSpawned`, `HostileThreat`
- **Action:** Summons Fyros outpost guards, equips halberd, and assumes defensive stance.
- **Dialogue:** *"ALARM! Mandibles breaching the surface! Outpost militia to arms!"*

## Method 5: IdlePatrol
- **Trigger Stimuli:** `Default`, `LowStimulus`
- **Action:** Inspects lumber piles and surveys the horizon.
- **Dialogue:** *"Keep your blades sharp and your eyes on the sand dunes."*
